Version: 1.3.0
Date: 21. 10. 2026
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed Technology:add_pack() changing positional {name, count} science ingredients into named ingredients and losing the intended research cost.
    - Existing technology science packs now retain their original positional or named structure when upgraded, while their amounts increase correctly.
    - Corrected the recipe output inspection test fixture, which previously contradicted an earlier test's recipe changes.
  Graphics:
    - Added the new Razi's Realm dragon mascot thumbnail with simplified RaziLib branding.
  Testing:
    - Added regression coverage for both research science ingredient formats and repeated science-pack upgrades.
  Compatibility:
    - Existing RaziLib APIs remain intact; no gameplay changes or mandatory Space Age dependency.
    - Settings utilities remain opt-in and do not automatically claim other mods' settings or change their order.
